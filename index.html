About · the human behind the mammoth

I watched AI take the gigs I used to win. So I learned to build with it.

Jack Fentress Founder & AIBMM Executive Coach

I started out as a freelance marketer and copywriter. Brand guides, landing pages, lead magnets, campaigns, etc. Mostly for sustainability and ESG companies, back in 2021. Then one day I opened ChatGPT for the first time, typed a prompt, and watched it do a version of my job in about four seconds. I'm not going to lie to you: that was a weird afternoon.

I could've dug in, fought it, and resisted the AI movement. Instead I got curious. And the deeper I went, the more familiar it felt. Reverse-engineering, workflows, big-picture vision, and efficiency applications. All skills I had already developed. The difference? Better tools. Same thinking.

So while everyone was bracing for AI to end the world, I started learning to drive it. And I found the gap: businesses need someone who gets how their operations actually run, then builds the systems that help their growth compound.

That's Jolly Mammoth. We advise and build, starting with your business, not the tech. HQ'd in Eau Claire, working with contractors and service businesses across Wisconsin. If you're nervous about what AI means for your company, I get it. I was on the wrong end of it years ago. Today, my goal is to help business owners feel confident to take on the shifting business landscape.

Before you ask

Questions we get every week.

How long until I see results?

Most businesses see the first wins quickly. Faster follow-up, cleaner handoffs, fewer dropped leads, and clearer visibility in the first few weeks. The compounding part shows up over the next 60 to 90 days as the system starts running without you.

Do I have to learn the system?

No. We build and install it, then show you the few views that matter. Your job is to run the business. The system handles the busywork.

Is there a contract?

No long term commitment. You stay month to month because the system is working, not because you're locked in. If you ever step away, you keep the infrastructure we built.

What if I already use some marketing tools?

That is normal. We do not rip everything out on day one. We connect what is worth keeping and replace the parts causing dropped leads, slow response, or messy operations.

Do you work with my industry?

We work best with contractors and service businesses. If you sell work that requires calls, estimates, scheduling, and follow-through, the system fits.

What if it doesn't work for me?

That is why we start with a short strategy call. We back every install with a satisfaction guarantee and our Jolly in 90 promise. If you are not seeing the system working in the first 90 days, we make it right.
